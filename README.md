## About HeRoLab

The **Heterogeneous Robotics Research Lab (HeRoLab)** at the University of Georgia develops algorithms, systems, and open-source technologies for teams of autonomous robots operating in complex real-world environments.

Our research investigates how heterogeneous robots can **communicate, coordinate, learn, adapt, and cooperate** while dealing with limited sensing, uncertain communication, distributed information, dynamic environments, and human interaction.

Our work spans foundational research in multi-agent systems as well as applications in:

- Multi-Robot and Swarm Robotics
- Distributed Coordination and Control
- Networked Robotics and Robot Communication
- Multi-Robot Localization
- Robot Learning and Multi-Agent AI
- Exploration, Mapping, and Informative Path Planning
- Human–Robot Interaction and Teleoperation
- Search-and-Rescue Robotics
- Agricultural and Field Robotics
- Robotics for Challenging and Hazardous Environments

> **Our vision:** Enable intelligent, resilient, and robust heterogeneous robot teams capable of autonomous cooperation, communication, and interaction in complex environments.

#  Research Themes

## Multi-Robot Coordination & Distributed Autonomy

We develop decentralized methods that enable robot teams to make collective decisions using local sensing, communication, and computation.

Topics include:

`distributed control` · `task allocation` · `consensus` · `formation control` · `coverage` · `connectivity maintenance` · `multi-agent planning`

---

## Networked Robotics

Communication is treated as an integral part of robot autonomy rather than an external infrastructure.

Our research investigates the interaction between:

**robot motion ↔ wireless communication ↔ distributed decision making**

Topics include:

`wireless robotics` · `communication-aware planning` · `Wi-Fi sensing` · `network prediction` · `connectivity control`

---

## Swarm Robotics

We investigate how simple local interactions between robots can produce scalable collective behavior.

Topics include:

`collective intelligence` · `emergent behavior` · `distributed coordination` · `swarm control` · `swarm hardware`

---

## Multi-Robot Perception, Localization & Exploration

Our research examines how multiple robots can collectively understand environments under limited sensing and uncertain information.

Topics include:

`multi-robot localization` · `RSSI localization` · `SLAM` · `exploration` · `mapping` · `informative path planning`

---

## AI & Learning for Multi-Agent Robotics

We investigate how learning and AI can augment distributed robotic systems while preserving robustness, adaptability, and interpretability.

Topics include:

`multi-agent learning` · `robot learning` · `semantic reasoning` · `agentic AI` · `foundation models for robotics`

---

## Human–Robot Interaction

We develop methods for intuitive, safe, and effective interaction between humans and autonomous robot systems.

Topics include:

`teleoperation` · `shared autonomy` · `robot safety` · `human-swarm interaction` · `physical HRI`
